# Agent Spec: GrowthScout AI Multi-Agent Platform

## Overview
GrowthScout AI is a multi-agent platform designed to discover local businesses, analyze their web presence (SEO, tech stack, page speed, social presence), identify growth opportunities, calculate explainable Opportunity Scores, and compile comprehensive Growth Intelligence Reports (with personalized outreach proposals as a derived capability).

The platform coordinates five specialized ADK agents:
1.  **Orchestrator Agent**: Manages user intent, routes tasks, compiles multi-agent responses, and requests Human-in-the-Loop approval.
2.  **Business Discovery Agent**: Interfaces with Google Maps and local search MCP tools to retrieve lists of SMB targets.
3.  **Website Analysis Agent**: Audits target site performance, meta tags, and tech footprints using scraping tools.
4.  **Opportunity Agent**: Evaluates digital gaps, calculates explainable opportunity scores, and maps technical findings to business outcomes.
5.  5. **Growth Intelligence Agent**: Synthesizes audits, scores, competitive findings, and opportunity analysis into a master Growth Intelligence Report, with outreach drafts generated as a secondary output.

---

## Example Use Cases

### Scenario A: Full Discovery and Strategy Campaign
*   **User Input**: "Find dental clinics in Austin, Texas that don't have online booking, analyze their sites, and create a growth report pitching online scheduling."
*   **Execution Sequence**:
    1.  `Orchestrator` receives the request.
    2.  `Business Discovery Agent` queries local search tools for dentistry SMBs in Austin.
    3.  `Website Analysis Agent` crawls the websites of the discovered dental clinics.
    4.  `Opportunity Agent` parses the crawl results to check for scheduling widgets, forms, and mobile performance, linking them to business outcomes and scores.
    5. `Growth Intelligence Agent` compiles a comprehensive Growth Intelligence Report detailing the clinic's speed score, lack of booking widgets, competitive positioning, opportunity scores, and recommended growth actions, with outreach content generated as a secondary artifact.
    6.  `Orchestrator` requests review.
*   **Expected Output**: A comprehensive Growth Intelligence Report containing an executive summary, competitive audits, explainable opportunity scores, and derived outreach proposals.

## Constraints & Safety Rules

1.  **Strict Scraping Compliance**: The `web_page_fetcher` must strictly follow target website `robots.txt` directives. Rate limiting must be enforced (maximum 1 request per 2 seconds per domain).
2.  **PII Privacy Guardrail**: The agents must redact user contact details and internal agent metadata from final proposals unless explicitly approved by the user.
3.  **No Unverified Recommendations**: The Opportunity Agent must never hallucinate tech stacks. If a widget's presence is ambiguous, it must flag the opportunity as "Requires Manual Verification."
4.  **No Automated Outreach**: No outreach emails may be sent directly. All outputs are proposals to be reviewed and copied by the user.

---

## Success Criteria

1.  **Task Success Rate**: Over 90% of execution runs must successfully identify at least 3 qualifying local businesses, analyze their webpresence, and generate explainable growth recommendations matching user parameters.
2.  **Factual Grounding**: 100% of generated Growth Intelligence Reports must accurately state the target business name, website URL, and verified technical characteristics without hallucinating technical gaps.
3.  **Response Latency**: Core report generation pipelines must respond under 30 seconds for batch discovery and audit runs.
4.  **Report Quality Metric**: Reports must achieve a rating of 4/5 or higher on the `final_response_quality` metric for business impact analysis and explainability of opportunity scores.
